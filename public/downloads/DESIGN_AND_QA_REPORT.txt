EDITORIAL / PEDAGOGICAL OBSERVATIONS
1. The supplied material is a foundation/bridge curriculum labelled A1+ → A2 Bridge. It should not be described as officially aligned to a national government syllabus unless a current official syllabus is supplied and mapped.
2. The grade 7 student book starts with letter recognition and progresses into phonics and basic language. This is a suitable foundation direction for learners with weak literacy, but teachers should administer a short entry diagnostic before pacing the sequence.
3. The grade 8 book moves into personal information, routines, school/community, food/health, past experiences, future plans, problem solving, and portfolio work. The teacher guide itself cautions teachers not to assume every learner has mastered grade 7 basics.
4. Some lesson pages are intentionally sparse because the source lessons contain short task sets. The redesign preserves the source educational wording rather than inventing extra exercises or changing answers. A future content-development pass can expand student practice and add authentic visuals/activity cards.
5. Teacher guides are bilingual (Arabic/English) and student books are primarily English. This distinction has been retained.
6. No separate audio tracks or official government syllabus map were supplied in the package. These can be added or mapped in a later development stage.

SCOPE / LIMITATION
This revision updates the visual identity, covers, location line, and page-edge branding while preserving the educational content and pagination of the supplied PDFs. It is not a full rewrite or external standards accreditation. A full line-by-line language proofread and syllabus-alignment pass has not been claimed in this design revision. It should be completed against the official source curriculum before formal ministry submission or large-scale printing.
